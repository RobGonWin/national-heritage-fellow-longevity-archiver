# Public-loop constraints

Use public/synthetic evidence only. No family records, GEDCOM, DNA data, authenticated captures, local source paths, private hashes or credentials.
Retain the 2026-10-23 historical cutoff, approximate dates, source dependence and unresolved collective boundaries.
Search results are leads. A screening cap is not an attained age. Display order is not a universal longevity rank.
Image attribution does not establish reuse rights; unlicensed image files stay in ignored local review staging.
Stop on a privacy finding, unsupported claim, failed validation, unclear source provenance or third failed attempt.
Publication requires explicit review of the exact candidate and its release checks. Default work is local review.
